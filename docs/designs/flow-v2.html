Navigation as a game. Worlds are bento tiles you "enter"; problems are cards you flip or swipe; the full problem is a short run of bite cards with a progress bar. Back always returns to the world, never to the top.
Shine. Every tile and card is card stock with a glossy top-left sheen and a pressable edge. Each industry owns one colour and one croodle mascot, reused on every screen inside that world.
Reading load. Brief card: question, one line of why, four chips. Full problem: one section per bite, at most three facts, each with its source as a pill.
Nudges. One per screen, from the world's mascot, dry rather than loud. Tell me if the jokes should be sharper or softer.
