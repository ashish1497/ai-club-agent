# 21. Do AI tutors produce learning that lasts?

## The open question

Do AI-tutor gains persist after 6–12 months, and for which learners, languages and schools in India?

## Why it matters

Large investments in AI tutoring assume lasting learning gains.

## What is known

- Short-term trials look positive.

## What is unknown

- Persistence of gains.
- Separating novelty effects from real learning.

## Possible angles

- **Theory:** causal inference with delayed outcomes
- **Practice (no lab):** a pre-registered study design with an edtech partner
