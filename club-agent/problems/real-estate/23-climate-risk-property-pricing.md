# 23. Is climate risk priced into property?

## The open question

Are property-level climate-risk scores reflected in valuations and mortgage terms, and can those scores be validated?

## Why it matters

Mispriced climate risk transfers losses to buyers, lenders and insurers.

## Who cares

- Mortgage lenders' credit and risk teams
- Property insurers and reinsurers
- Real-estate developers, valuers and homebuyer advocates

## In scope

- Testing whether public flood-hazard information shows up in Indian residential prices or loan terms, using public data
- Comparing how different hazard maps or risk scores rate the same locations
- Reviewing what Indian financial regulators require lenders to disclose about climate risk

## Out of scope

- Valuing specific properties or giving buy/sell or lending advice
- Building a commercial risk-scoring product
- Running physical flood models from scratch
- Making public claims about named properties or developers

## What is known

- US homes exposed to projected sea-level rise sold for about 7% less than comparable unexposed homes equidistant from the beach, a discount that grew over time. ([Disaster on the horizon: The price effect of sea level rise (Journal of Financial Economics, 2019)](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:jfinec:v:134:y:2019:i:2:p:253-272))
- RBI's 2024 draft climate disclosure framework states that inadequate information about climate-related financial risks can lead to mispricing of assets and misallocation of capital. ([RBI Draft Disclosure Framework on Climate-related Financial Risks, 2024](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/bs_viewcontent.aspx?Id=4393))
- Florida's attorney general sued First Street in October 2026 alleging its property flood scores deviate from FEMA floodplain maps; First Street says FEMA maps work at community level while it scores individual properties. ([ESG Dive: Florida AG sues climate analytics provider First Street over home risk scores (Nov 2026)](https://www.esgdive.com/news/florida-ag-sues-climate-analytics-provider-first-street-over-home-risk-scor/873473/))

## What is unknown

- Building-level accuracy where models disagree.
- Whether markets price risk before insurers retreat.

## Key terms

- **Hedonic pricing**: Explaining a property's price as the sum of the values of its features, such as size, location and hazard exposure.
- **Capitalisation of risk**: The extent to which an expected future loss is already reflected in today's price.
- **Property-level climate risk score**: A rating of how exposed a single building is to hazards like flood or heat, usually from a private model.
- **Return period**: How rare a flood is, e.g. a 1-in-100-year flood has a 1% chance each year.
- **Physical vs transition risk**: Losses from hazards themselves versus losses from policy and market shifts toward low carbon.
