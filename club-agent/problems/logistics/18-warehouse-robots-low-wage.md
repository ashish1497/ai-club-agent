# 18. Do warehouse robot swarms pay off where labour is cheap?

## The open question

Can modular robot swarms earn back their cost in small, brownfield Indian warehouses?

## Why it matters

Automation economics proven in high-wage markets may not transfer.

## Who cares

- Operations head at an Indian 3PL or e-commerce fulfilment company
- Investor or finance lead evaluating warehouse automation capex
- Labour-policy researcher or workforce planner

## In scope

- Total cost of ownership of modular or swarm robots versus manual operations in small, brownfield Indian warehouses
- Effects on jobs, wages and working conditions as an outcome, not an afterthought
- Indian wage levels, space constraints and volume variability

## Out of scope

- Designing or building robot hardware
- Vendor selection or procurement advice for a specific company
- Legal advice on labour law

## What is known

- IFR reported 102,900 professional service robots for transportation and logistics sold in 2024, up 14% and the largest application class. ([IFR - World Robotics 2025, Service Robots press release](https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/news/service-robots-see-global-growth-boom))
- A DPIIT-NCAER study estimated India's logistics cost at 7.97% of GDP in 2023-24. ([NCAER - Logistics Cost in India (September 2025)](https://ncaer.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NCAER_Logistic_Study_Report_Sept2025.pdf))
- In Chinese firm data, a 10% minimum-wage rise in 2008-2012 raised the probability of robot adoption by about 0.11 percentage points against a 0.19% average, with little effect in 2004-2007. ([Labor costs and the adoption of robots in China (Fan, Hu and Tang, JEBO 2021)](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:jeborg:v:186:y:2021:i:c:p:608-631))
- Pune-based Unbox Robotics, which makes modular swarm-robotic sortation systems, raised a $28M (about Rs 243 crore) Series B in January 2026. ([Pulse 2.0 - Unbox Robotics $28 Million Series B](https://pulse2.com/unbox-robotics-28-million-series-b/))

## What is unknown

- Returns in low-wage, space-constrained settings.

## Key terms

- **Total cost of ownership (TCO)**: All costs of a system over its life: purchase, installation, software, maintenance, downtime and staff.
- **Brownfield warehouse**: An existing building being retrofitted, rather than a new one designed for automation.
- **3PL**: Third-party logistics company that runs warehousing or delivery for other firms.
- **Swarm robotics**: Many small robots coordinated by software to do a task together, such as sorting parcels.
- **Payback period**: How long until savings from an investment cover its cost.
