# 28. How much carbon does crushed rock really remove?

## The open question

How much CO₂ does basalt spread on farmland actually remove, and can that be verified cheaply at scale?

## Why it matters

Carbon removal markets need trustworthy measurement.

## Who cares

- Corporate carbon-removal buyers and buyer coalitions
- Carbon registries and verifiers writing measurement rules
- Agri-input companies and farmer organisations in basalt regions such as the Deccan

## In scope

- How registries require removals to be measured and where they differ
- Sources of uncertainty and loss between field and ocean
- Cost of sampling and verification per tonne and how it scales

## Out of scope

- Spreading rock or running field trials
- Agronomic or soil-safety advice to farmers
- Buying or trading carbon credits

## What is known

- Frontier buyers agreed to pay Eion $33M to remove 78,707 tonnes of CO2 between 2027 and 2030 using olivine on farmland. ([Frontier: Frontier buyers sign $33M in offtake agreements with Eion](https://frontierclimate.com/writing/eion))
- Mati Carbon, which spreads basalt on smallholder farms in India, won the $50M XPRIZE Carbon Removal grand prize in April 2025. ([XPRIZE: Awards $100M Prize for Carbon Removal Solutions](https://go.xprize.org/news/xprize-makes-history-awards-100m-prize-for-groundbreaking-carbon-removal-solutions))
- Isometric's agriculture enhanced-weathering protocol requires every project to design a sampling plan that directly measures initial weathering. ([Isometric: Enhanced Weathering in Agriculture protocol](https://registry.isometric.com/protocol/enhanced-weathering-agriculture))
- Researchers note that reliable, scalable measurement of weathering in soil is hard and no single method has consensus. ([Mass-Balance MRV for Carbon Dioxide Removal by Enhanced Rock Weathering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01949))

## What is unknown

- Losses in soils and rivers.
- Cost of measurement at scale.

## Key terms

- **Enhanced rock weathering (ERW)**: Spreading crushed silicate rock on land so it reacts with CO2 faster than it would naturally.
- **MRV**: Measurement, reporting and verification: how removed carbon is counted and checked.
- **Offtake agreement**: A contract to buy future carbon removal at an agreed price.
- **Bicarbonate**: The dissolved form in which weathering stores carbon, carried by water toward the ocean.
- **Mass balance**: Estimating removal by tracking how much rock-derived material disappears from soil over time.
