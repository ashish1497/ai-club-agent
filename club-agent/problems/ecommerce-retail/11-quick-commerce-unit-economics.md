# 11. Can 10-minute delivery ever profit without discounts?

## The open question

At what dark-store density and order mix does quick commerce break even without discounting, and is that stable with three or more players in the same pin codes?

## Why it matters

Billions are being invested on the assumption that it eventually will.

## Who cares

- Strategy or finance lead at a quick-commerce company
- Equity research analyst or venture investor covering Indian consumer internet
- Category manager at an FMCG brand deciding how much to invest in quick-commerce channels

## In scope

- Unit economics of a dark store as a function of order density, basket size and mix
- Whether break-even holds without discounting or heavy marketing spend
- How profitability changes when three or more players overlap in the same pin codes
- Models built only from public filings, shareholder letters and published data

## Out of scope

- Investment recommendations on listed or pre-IPO companies
- Obtaining confidential company or pin-code data
- Legal advice on competition law or the dark-pattern guidelines
- Operating or piloting a delivery service

## What is known

- Zepto's updated DRHP (June 2026) shows FY26 operating revenue of about Rs 22,624 crore and a net loss of about Rs 5,905 crore, with 1,139 dark stores as of 31 March 2026 and Rs 1,629 crore earmarked for 1,904 more by FY30. ([Inc42: Zepto files UDRHP, plans Rs 8,010 Cr fresh issue](https://inc42.com/?p=560728))
- Blinkit reported an adjusted EBITDA profit of Rs 4 crore in Q3 FY26, up from a Rs 156 crore loss in Q2 FY26, with 2,027 dark stores at quarter-end. ([Inc42: Eternal Q3 - Blinkit, Hyperpure achieve adjusted EBITDA profitability](https://inc42.com/?p=545894))
- Zepto raised a round led by US pension fund CalPERS in October 2025, mixing primary and secondary capital. ([TechCrunch: Zepto raises $450 million at $7 billion valuation](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/16/zepto-raises-450-million-at-7-billion-valuation-as-indian-quick-commerce-market-heats-up))

## What is unknown

- Steady-state profitability.
- How market structure settles.

## Key terms

- **Dark store**: A small warehouse closed to walk-in shoppers, used only to pick and pack online orders for fast local delivery.
- **Contribution margin**: What an order earns after paying for the goods, delivery, packing and discounts, before fixed company costs.
- **Adjusted EBITDA**: Operating profit before interest, tax, depreciation and some company-defined items; a common but non-standard profitability measure.
- **Average order value (AOV)**: The average rupee value of a single order.
- **DRHP / UDRHP**: Draft (and updated draft) red herring prospectus: the detailed document a company files with SEBI before an IPO.
